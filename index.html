OPERATIVE IDENTITIES

Claim these roles to use their abilities. Others can challenge you!

💰

BUSINESSMAN

FREE ABILITY

Take 4 coins from the bank. Cannot be blocked or challenged.

📊

TAXMAN

FREE ABILITY

Tax all players with 7+ coins (takes 1 from each). Can block Support from Bank.

💣

SABOTEUR

COSTS 3 COINS

Pay 3 coins to eliminate one card from any player. Target must discard a card.

🎯

MARSHAL

COSTS 4 COINS

Pay 4 coins to guess a player's role. If correct, they lose a card.

🔍

INSPECTOR

FREE ABILITY

Peek at one of a target's cards. You may choose to swap with one of yours.

🔄

POLITICIEN

FREE ABILITY

Discard all your alive cards and draw new ones from the deck.

🪙

THIEF

FREE ABILITY

Steal up to 3 coins from any player. Cannot be blocked.

FIELD MANUAL

HOW TO PLAY

1

GATHER OPERATIVES

Gather 2-9 players around one device. Each player receives 2-3 secret identity cards.

2

TAKE YOUR TURN

On your turn, choose an action: collect income, claim a role, or use special abilities.

3

BLUFF OR CHALLENGE

Anyone can yell "LIAR!" to challenge a claim. But if they're wrong, they lose a card!

4

SURVIVE

Be the last operative standing with at least one identity card still in play.
