Every round, operatives hold secret role cards. You can claim any ability at any time. Will your rivals let you get away with it, or call your bluff?

#01 FREE ABILITY
UNSTOPPABLE PROFIT

BUSINESSMAN

Take 4 coins straight from the central bank. This ability cannot be blocked by standard defenses.

Tactical Note: Hoard coins quickly to reach the 7-Kill protocol, but watch out for greedy Taxmen!
#02 FREE ABILITY
ECONOMIC CONTROL

TAXMAN

Taxes all wealthy players with 7+ coins (takes 1 from each). Can also block Support actions (+2) from the bank.

Tactical Note: Keep rivals starved of coins so they can never afford assassination strikes.
#03 COSTS 3 COINS
LETHAL STRIKE

ASSASSIN

Pay 3 coins to launch an assassination against any operative. Target must permanently discard one alive identity card.

Tactical Note: Vulnerable to Marshal counter-claims. Time your hit when rivals are low on defensive bluffs.
#04 COSTS 4 COINS
INTERROGATOR

MARSHAL

Pay 4 coins to interrogate a rival and guess their role. If you guess right, they lose a card. Guess wrong, they take your coins!

Tactical Note: Counters Assassins! Use behavioral deduction and previous claim patterns to nail targets.
#05 FREE ABILITY
ESPIONAGE

DETECTIVE

Secretly peek at one of a target's cards. You may choose to swap it with one of yours to steal their best weapon.

Tactical Note: Information is lethal. Knowing what someone has makes their bluffs effortless to crush.
#06 FREE ABILITY
SHUFFLE & RESET

POLITICIAN

Discard all your active cards into the deck and draw completely fresh identities. Resets your profile when cornered.

Tactical Note: When everyone thinks they know your hand, a Politician swap shatters their entire plan.
#07 FREE ABILITY
PICKPOCKET

THIEF

Steal up to 2 coins from any operative. Only another claimed Thief can counter and stop the robbery.

Tactical Note: Punish opponents who are saving up for big kills, while fueling your own bank.

HOW TO PLAY THE SYSTEM

Master the rules of deceit. Learn how every round unfolds from dealing to final elimination.

01

SETUP & ROLES

2 to 8 operatives gather around one phone. Everyone secretly checks their 3 dealt role cards. Coins are pooled in the bank.

02

TAKE ACTION

On your turn, choose: take Income (+1), Support (+2), claim a role's ability (like Taxman or Assassin), or launch a strike.

03

BLUFF OR CHALLENGE

You can claim any role even if you don't hold it. The challenge countdown starts: rivals can call "LIAR!" or let your claim pass.

04

CARD ELIMINATION

If caught lying, you discard an alive card. If the challenger was wrong, THEY lose a card. Be the last operative alive to win!

DEBRIEFING & INTEL

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about gameplay, offline modes, and rules.

What is The System and how does it play? ▼
The System is a social deduction bluffing card game for 2 to 8 players inspired by the classic Tunisian card game El Mekina. Every operative receives secret identity cards from a deck containing 7 unique roles. On your turn, you claim any role and trigger its ability—regardless of whether you actually have that card in your hand. Other operatives have a countdown window to challenge your claim by yelling "LIAR!". Caught liars lose an identity card, while false accusers also lose a card. The last player with cards remaining wins.
Can I play with my friends on a single phone without Wi-Fi? ▼
Yes! The core feature of The System is Local Pass-and-Play (2–8 players). You only need one smartphone or tablet passed around the group. The app hides secret cards behind private reveal buttons so no one sees each other's hand. It requires zero internet connection, making it perfect for road trips, parties, flights, and game nights.
Is there a solo mode against bots or online multiplayer? ▼
Yes. The System features:
  • Single Player vs AI Bots: Practice your bluffing psychology against smart AI opponents with adjustable difficulty settings.
  • Online 1v1 Multiplayer: Play against other operatives worldwide with real-time lobbies, live reactions/taunts, and ELO competitive ranking.
How does The System compare to Coup, Werewolf, or El Mekina? ▼
If you love the tense bluffing of Coup and the psychological deduction of Werewolf/Mafia, The System delivers a dynamic experience rooted in El Mekina: 3 secret cards per player for deeper tactical endurance, 7 distinct roles (Businessman, Taxman, Assassin, Marshal, Detective, Politician, Thief), the 7-Kill elimination protocol, and tactical Nine-Shield armor mechanics.
Is The System free to play and pay-to-win? ▼
The System is 100% free to play with zero pay-to-win elements. Every player has identical access to roles and probabilities. Victory relies strictly on your poker face, strategic reading of opponents, and risk management.
